Common questions

Everything you need to know.

Still have a question? Talk to our team.

What is an AI receptionist?+

An AI receptionist answers customer calls, holds a natural conversation, collects the information your business needs and completes actions such as booking an appointment.

Can Atlas use my existing phone number?+

Yes. Atlas can work with your existing business number, so customers do not need to change how they contact you.

Can Atlas book directly into my calendar?+

Yes. Atlas checks your actual availability and adds confirmed appointments directly to your connected calendar.

Can customers reschedule or cancel appointments?+

Yes. Atlas can identify an existing appointment and handle rescheduling or cancellation according to your rules.

What happens if Atlas cannot answer a question?+

You decide the fallback. Atlas can take a message, flag the call for follow-up or route the caller according to the process agreed for your business.

Can I control when Atlas answers calls?+

Yes. Atlas can answer every call, cover only busy periods, or operate outside your normal opening hours.

Does Atlas work outside business hours?+

Yes. Atlas can answer and book calls during evenings, weekends and other times when your team is unavailable.

Can Atlas be customised for my business?+

Yes. Atlas is configured around your services, locations, opening hours, availability and specific booking rules.
